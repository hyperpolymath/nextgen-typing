== Current State (Updated 2026-11-03)

`nextgen-typing` is a pure coordination and cross-project formal verification repository (`OQ-001`, PR #56). The former `rsr-template-repo` Idris2/Zig scaffold was retired in favour of cross-project proof witnesses under `verification/proofs/` and bidirectional gate control suites under `tests/`.

[width="100%",cols="34%,20%,46%",options="header"]
|===
|Category |Count |Details
|*Formal proof modules (`XP-1`)* |3 |`EchoTyping.agda` (Agda `--safe --without-K`), `EchoTyping.v` (Rocq 8.18), `EchoTyping.lean` (core Lean 4.15, zero Mathlib)
|*Proof-ledger bidirectional controls* |63 |`tests/proofs/run-ledger-controls.sh` (`PROOF-POLICY` §7 positive + negative controls for ledger, enrolment, witness receipts, and comment-aware escape-hatch scanner)
|*Kernel-assumption controls* |18 |`tests/proofs/run-kernel-assumption-controls.sh` (Coq `coqchk` + `Print Assumptions`, Lean `#print axioms` + `getModuleIdxFor?`, Agda `--safe --without-K`)
|*Workflow & antipattern tests* |2 |`tests/workflows/validate_workflows_test.sh` (29 workflows) + `tests/rsr_antipattern_heading_test.sh`
|*Aspect & structural gates* |4 |`tests/aspect_tests.sh`, `scripts/validate-template.sh`, `scripts/check-coordination-boundary.sh`, `scripts/check-pipeline-drift.sh`
|*E2E & benchmark harnesses* |2 |`tests/e2e/template_instantiation_test.sh` + `benches/template_bench.sh`
|===
