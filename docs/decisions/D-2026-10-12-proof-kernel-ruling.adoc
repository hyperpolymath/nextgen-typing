= D-2026-10-12 — Proof kernel for the type family
:revdate: 2026-10-12

== Status

Ruled by the owner, 2026-10-12, after a comparison recorded in the
accompanying session. Supersedes per-repo prover choice for the family.

== Decision

. *Agda `--safe --without-K` is the proof kernel of the type family*
  (echo-types, epistemic-types, residual-evidence-types, choreographic-types,
  secret-types, resource-algebra; absolute-zero's active CNO/OND/EchoBridge
  stack).
. *Idris2 is the ABI/runtime layer*, not a kernel candidate (QTT linearity is
  the right tool there; `believe_me`/`assert_total` make it the wrong one for
  theorems).
. *Lean 4 is provenance*: jtv keeps its existing proof layer; tropical-types'
  Lean and Isabelle developments remain as the Mathlib/AFP-facing record and
  are *ported*, not imported, into Agda where the family needs them.
. *Isabelle/HOL only for AFP submissions.* Coq, Mizar: archived (absolute-zero
  ruling 2026-10-10 stands).

== Evidence weighed

* Investment: ≈47.7k lines of Agda across the family, every file
  `--safe --without-K`, 3 postulates total; ≈6.4k active Lean; 7.1k Isabelle
  (tropical only); Idris2 almost entirely RSR ABI scaffolding.
* K: the equivalence-as-identity programme needs a kernel without UIP. Lean's
  Prop proof-irrelevance gives K by construction; Idris2 has no without-K
  mode; Agda has `--without-K` and `--cubical`.
* Discipline: `--safe` is the strictest mechanical falsifier in the estate and
  is already uniform.
* Cost: consolidating on Idris2 or Lean would mean re-proving the Agda
  corpus; not viable this quarter.

== Falsifier

resource-algebra (the first repo written under the ruling) had to reach a
postulate-free `ResourceAlgebra` record with three instances under the flags
within a week. It did on day one (`just check`, Agda 2.6.3), so the ruling
stands; it is revisited if a family theorem is later found unstateable under
`--without-K`.

== Consequences

* absolute-zero `LANGUAGE-POLICY.adoc` v1.6.0 ("Idris2 is the only
  formal-verification language") needs an exception record (see T1); the
  constitution's change procedure applies.
* katagoria's Idris2 research sketches remain the incubator's prototypes;
  graduation into a family repo means an Agda formalisation.
* Creusot and SPARK are implementation-verification questions for januskey's
  and jtv's Rust surfaces, outside this ruling.
