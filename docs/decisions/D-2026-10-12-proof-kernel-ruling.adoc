= D-2026-10-12 — Proof kernel for the type family
:revdate: 2026-10-12

== Status

Ruled by the owner, 2026-10-12, after a comparison recorded in the
accompanying session. Supersedes per-repo prover choice for the family.

== Decision

. *Agda `--safe --without-K` is the proof kernel of the type family*
  (echo-types, epistemic-types, residual-evidence-types, choreographic-types,
  secret-types, resource-algebra; absolute-zero's active CNO/OND/EchoBridge
  stack).
. *Idris2 is the ABI/runtime layer*, not a kernel candidate (QTT linearity is
  the right tool there; `believe_me`/`assert_total` make it the wrong one for
  theorems).
. *Lean 4 is provenance*: jtv keeps its existing proof layer; tropical-types'
  Lean and Isabelle developments remain as the Mathlib/AFP-facing record and
  are *ported*, not imported, into Agda where the family needs them.
. *Isabelle/HOL only for AFP submissions.* Coq, Mizar: archived (absolute-zero
  ruling 2026-10-10 stands).

== Evidence weighed

* Investment: the comparison reported ≈47.7k lines of Agda across the
  family; ≈6.4k active Lean; 7.1k Isabelle (tropical only); Idris2 almost
  entirely RSR ABI scaffolding. These estimates are not a safe-check receipt.
* Postulate inventory: at echo-types commit
  `e38fd465c4269ab9e9940802525630d7aae1e848`, three `postulate` blocks
  declare *six* assumptions:
** https://github.com/hyperpolymath/echo-types/blob/e38fd465c4269ab9e9940802525630d7aae1e848/proofs/agda/EchoImageFactorizationPropPostulated.agda[EchoImageFactorizationPropPostulated.agda]:
   `Trunc-pos`, `∣_∣-pos`, `is-prop-pos`, and `rec-pos` (one block).
** https://github.com/hyperpolymath/echo-types/blob/e38fd465c4269ab9e9940802525630d7aae1e848/proofs/agda/Ordinal/Buchholz/Fidelity.agda[Ordinal/Buchholz/Fidelity.agda]:
   `denotation` and `ordinal-upper-bound` (two blocks).
+
Both modules use `--without-K` only, are excluded from `All.agda` and
`Smoke.agda`, and are exempted by Echo's foundation guardrail. They are
outside the `--safe --without-K` checks; `--safe` rejects these postulates.
This is not a family-wide total: choreographic-types also contains the
https://github.com/hyperpolymath/choreographic-types/blob/a0f647531a8cacad10294703ade2b6c8cec3255c/applications/rapidnj-two-thread.agda[standalone RapidNJ interface/conjecture sketch],
with postulates and no safe-check flags, outside its repository build.
The earlier “3 postulates total” and “every file” claims are withdrawn.
The link:../TYPE-CONNECTIONS.adoc[existing proof receipts] cover bounded
checks; the complete constituent suites were not rerun for this record.
* K: the equivalence-as-identity programme needs a kernel without UIP. Lean's
  Prop proof-irrelevance gives K by construction; Idris2 has no without-K
  mode; Agda has `--without-K` and `--cubical`.
* Discipline: `--safe --without-K` is required for the selected proof kernel.
  This requirement does not establish uniform compliance across exploratory
  files or constitute an estate-wide verification result.
* Cost: consolidating on Idris2 or Lean would mean re-proving the Agda
  corpus; not viable this quarter.

== Falsifier

resource-algebra (the first repo written under the ruling) had to reach a
postulate-free `ResourceAlgebra` record with three instances under the flags
within a week. It did on day one (`just check`, Agda 2.6.3), so the ruling
stands; it is revisited if a family theorem is later found unstateable under
`--without-K`.
