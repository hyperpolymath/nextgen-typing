= Policy reconciliation: where the estate's proof rules disagree, and which reading binds
:revdate: 2026-11-01
:status: ADOPTED for this repository; upstream items are proposals pending owner ruling
:toc:

A consistency audit of this repository's proof lane against the estate policies
found not one authority but five, and they do not say the same thing. Each
disagreement below has a weak reading and a strict reading. This record names the
reading this repository adopts, and what the upstream document would have to say
for the strict reading to be the only one available.

Nothing here weakens a gate. Where the strict reading is ours to implement it is
implemented and controlled; where it is not, it is written as an owner-ruling
proposal rather than paraphrased into compliance.

PROOF-POLICY itself says which of these it defers to: escape hatches stay with
TRUSTED-BASE-REDUCTION-POLICY, and the canonical token list is the one in
README-EXPLAINME-STANDARD. It says nothing about mathesis, and mathesis says
nothing about the standards repository. That silence is where the weak readings
live.

|A1 |PROOF-POLICY §2.1 (2026-11-01) says first-party Agda entrypoints *shall* use
`--safe --without-K --cubical-compatible`. TRUSTED-BASE §"Why the prover

|A2 |mathesis §5 offers "Full `--cubical`" as a Grade-3 configuration. §2.1

|Upstream (mathesis) + here (the profile would be recorded in the ledger).
|A3 |mathesis §5: 2i = translated to **one** alternative system. PROOF-POLICY §2:
rechecked in Rocq **and** Lean. *Weak:* claim 2i with one leg and let a reader

|Upstream (mathesis) + here (already stated)

|Lineage lives on the *receipt*, not on the status token: `historical_evidence[].
state` + reason. The closed vocabulary stays closed.
|Implemented here (gate refuses an unmarked archived receipt). Upstream: both

|A5 |README-EXPLAINME §5 requires the README scorecard "Proofs" row to show "the

|Ratios of *rows* are counts with a stated denominator, not confidence. No

|A6 |Two ledger shapes: a markdown table (README-EXPLAINME §5) and a machine

|A8 |`proven-with-postulates` defers to TRUSTED-BASE(c), which cites MathLib4 as

|The two clauses govern different things: §2 forbids the *dependency*; TRB(c)

and must not enter through the library.
|Upstream: one sentence in both.
|A9 |§6 requires proof toolchains "through the repo's Guix manifest or its

|Upstream (mathesis + an owner ruling in the policy).
|A11 |§7 requires that "a gate that enforces this policy cites this file" — but

|A citation names repository, path and the full 40-character commit it was read

|A12 |§4: "The theorem is complete when the Agda proof is PROVEN or

|A waiver never buys a rung: a theorem with a waived leg has no independent

== The local document that was doing the same thing

`PROOF-NEEDS.adoc` declared *Tier T5 — Exempt* and stopped. A reader — or an
agent — could take that as "the proof policy does not apply to this repo", which
would have excused the open Rocq and Lean legs. It now states the other reading:
exemption is from the RSR template's *mandated* obligations, and hosting a proof
makes this repository proof-bearing for the trusted-base policy and for §7. See
the file.

== What was fixed here rather than argued

* `docs/proof-debt.md` added, and `.trusted-base-ignore` for the instrument's
  planted fixtures. The estate checker now reports
  `core_undocumented=0` — before, `governance-reusable.yml`'s `trusted-base` job
  failed this repository for having an escape hatch and no register.
* `scripts/proof-scan-dangerous.sh` widened to the estate's **core tier**
  (Coq `Axiom`, Lean `axiom`, Idris2 `really_believe_me`/`partial`, F* `assume
  val`/`admit_p`, `unsafePerformIO`), and the ledger gate no longer keeps a
  second, narrower copy of the pattern list — it delegates. One list, one
  meaning: a marker cannot be caught by one gate and missed by the other.
  Controls 29 and 30 plant a Coq `Axiom` and remove the debt register.
* §2.1's canonical invocation, §5's closed vocabulary, §6's absence of a
  deviation mechanism, and A11's pin are reflected in the gate and the ledger.
* `scripts/check-proofs.sh` (issue #131) now executes kernel-level assumption
  queries for every gated Rocq (`coqchk -silent -o` + generated
  `Print Assumptions` probe) and Lean (`import` + `#print axioms` probe) module,
  comparing against `LEDGER.json` `legs[].axioms`, unconditionally rejecting
  `sorryAx`, writing verbatim assumption receipts (`witness-coq.txt`,
  `witness-lean4.txt`), and exercising both directions across `K6`–`K15` in
  `tests/proofs/run-controls.sh`.

== What is still wrong, and is not this repository's to fix

A1–A3, A5, A6, A8–A11 are owner-ruling items in the standards repository or in
mathesis. They are filed with the proposed strict text rather than described
here: standards#1389 carries A1, A6, A7, A8, A9, A11, A12; mathesis#11 carries
A2, A3, A4, A5, A10. Until they land, either reading can be defended by citation, and the
strict treatment depends on each repository choosing it. This repository chooses
it, records the choice, and gates it — which is the most a single repo can do.

The one substantive gap left here is A9: the proof lane installs Rocq and Lean
from the package index and the release archive with asserted versions and a
recorded digest, and the policy has no lawful way to acknowledge that. Closing it
by compliance rather than by exemption means a Guix manifest (or the container)
carrying both provers, with the lane installing from it.
