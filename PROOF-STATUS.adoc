== Proof Status — nextgen-typing

____
Coordination repo. The only proofs tracked here are *cross-project*
proofs (spanning ≥2 constituent repos). Single-project proof status is
tracked in the owning repos. Requirements: `+PROOF-NEEDS.adoc+`.
____

The machine-readable record of every proof is
`+verification/proofs/LEDGER.json+`. This page is a *summary of that ledger*;
where the two disagree the ledger is authoritative and
`+just proof-ledger-check+` fails. Nothing here is a percentage, a confidence
estimate, or a count of "done" work: mathesis §6 requires the exact form —
Open / Claimed, the grade, the axiom list, and the commit the receipt was taken
at.

`+XP-1+` states that the pipeline's information-losing typing operations
(AffineScript affine weakening; refinement erasure into the
typed-wasm/kernel layer) are instances of echo-types' fiber construction.

=== What the grade means

mathesis `+estate-mathematical-architecture+` §5 defines the rungs. *Grade 3*
is the lowest: a proof carried by Agda alone, under `+--safe --without-K+`, and
*not translated* to another prover. That is exactly and only what is
established here.

The rungs above it (`+2i+` = one independent prover, `+2ii+` = two
fundamentally distinct architectures, `+1+` = machine-checked through a
verified checker API) are *not established*. They are named here only so the
gap is unmistakable: *no leg other than Agda has been proven for XP-1.*

* `+2i+` needs exactly one independent system (Lean 4 or Rocq) to carry the
  statement. Neither does.
* `+2ii+` needs two, *and* the family caveat applies: Agda, Rocq and Lean are
  all dependently-typed (DTT/CIC). Translating between them does not produce a
  second architecture, so `+2ii+` is unreachable from the Agda/Rocq/Lean
  family alone — it would need a non-DTT leg such as Isabelle/HOL.
* `+1+` needs the statement checked through a verified checker API (AXLE for
  Lean, coq-lsp's REST surface for Rocq, isabelle-server). No such surface is
  wired up.

=== The recheck obligation, stated plainly

`+standards/3-practice/PROOF-POLICY.adoc+` §1 makes Agda the source of truth
for theory proofs and §2 makes Rocq *and* Lean the recheck legs: each theorem
must be *transported* (Dedukti/Lambdapi) or *re-proved by hand*, the route and
the statement comparison recorded, and no Mathlib allowed. **That obligation is
currently unmet for XP-1.** Both legs are recorded `+open+` in the ledger, with
the blocker that stops each one, and the gate prints them as `+POLICY GAP+`
lines on every run. An `+open+` leg is a legal state in the §5 vocabulary; it
is not verification, and no part of this page or of CI claims that it is.

[cols="14%,30%,56%",options="header",]

|Leg |Status |What would have to happen (the recorded blocker)
|Rocq |*open* |Transport the Agda term through Dedukti/Lambdapi (recording the
statement comparison and the `+Print Assumptions+` output), or re-prove the
statement by hand in Rocq. No `+TypeSafety.v+`-style witness exists here yet —
the Rocq MANIFEST is a header explaining this, and

`+sorryAx+` must not appear.
|===

=== Assumptions and axiom tracking

mathesis §6 requires the axioms behind a proof to be tracked, not assumed away:
`+#print axioms+` in Lean, `+Print Assumptions+` in Rocq. Those are kernel
queries, and they are the only accepted evidence that a compiled witness is
closed — compiling is not: Rocq accepts `+Admitted+` and Lean accepts `+sorry+`
with exit status 0. Since no witness exists on either leg there is no axiom
list for them yet; recording one would be fabrication.

=== Enforcement (CI)

Nothing on this page is trusted because it is written here. Each claim is
gated, and every gate refuses rather than skips:

[cols="30%,70%",options="header",]
|===
|Guard |Behaviour
|`+just proof-ledger-check+`
(`+scripts/check-proof-ledger.sh+`)
|Audits `+LEDGER.json+` against PROOF-POLICY §5 and mathesis §5-§6: schema and
closed status vocabulary; a grade the proven legs do not support (a moved leg
with no artifact, no MANIFEST entry, no route, no statement comparison or no
axiom list); `+proven+` carrying axioms; `+proven-with-postulates+` without
them; a Mathlib import; escape hatches; undeclared axioms; registry drift
between the MANIFESTs, `+ALLOWED_PROOFS+` and the files on disk; a witness on
disk that neither the ledger nor a MANIFEST claims; and this page claiming a
grade the ledger does not record. Open legs are printed as `+POLICY GAP+`, and
are never counted as verification.

|`+just proof-witness-check+`
|The CI target: the enrolment and banned-pattern gates, the ledger audit, and
*every recheck leg the ledger declares* (`+proof-check-coq+`, `+proof-check-lean4+`). Today both legs are declared open,
so the manifests compile nothing and the gate says so — the lane is green on
"the ledger is honest", never on "the theorem is verified".

|`+tests/proofs/run-controls.sh+`
|PROOF-POLICY §7 requires a gate to be verified *both ways*. These controls
feed the instrument a closed Rocq proof and a closed Lean proof (must be
accepted, `+Print Assumptions+` / `+#print axioms+` must report them closed and
axiom-free), a proof closed with `+Admitted+` and one closed with `+sorry+`
(must be rejected), a ledger claiming a grade with no leg behind it, and a
witness planted on disk that no ledger row claims. A control that stops
failing is a broken instrument, and the run goes red. Where `+coqc+` or
`+lean+` is absent the controls report **BLOCKED**, never a pass.

|`+just proof-enrolment+`
(`+scripts/check-proof-enrolment.sh+`)
|Parses `+[verification].allowed-proofs+` (placement.a2ml) and
`+ALLOWED_PROOFS+` (`+scripts/check-coordination-boundary.sh+`) and requires
them to agree. Fails when a proof source is unlisted, when a registered entry
has no file behind it, when zero proofs are enrolled, or when an `.agda` proof
loses `+--safe+`/`+--without-K+`.

|`+just proof-check-agda+` (and the Idris2/Lean4/Coq equivalents)
|Exits 2 (BLOCKED) when proofs for that prover are enrolled but the prover is
absent; exits 1 on any typecheck failure.

|`+just proof-scan-dangerous+`
(`+scripts/proof-scan-dangerous.sh+`)
|Comment-aware keyword scan for `+believe_me+`, `+assert_total+`,
`+postulate+`, `+sorry+`, `+Admitted+`, `+unsafeCoerce+`, `+Obj.magic+`. Fails
on any hit outside a comment, and fails when it scans zero files.

|`.github/workflows/proof-ci.yml`
|Hosted lane running `+just proof-witness-check+` (plus the Agda source leg and
the both-ways controls) on pushes/PRs that touch `+verification/proofs/**+`,
`+tests/proofs/**+`, the two registries, the gate scripts, `+Justfile+` or the
workflow itself. Pins Agda 2.8.0 (official binary, sha256-verified),
agda-stdlib v2.3 (tag + commit asserted), `+just+` 1.59.0, Rocq, Lean 4, and
echo-types / absolute-zero by commit. No `+continue-on-error+`, no
skip-on-missing-tool.
|===
