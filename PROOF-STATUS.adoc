== Proof Status — nextgen-typing

____
Coordination repo. The only proofs tracked here are *cross-project*
proofs (spanning ≥2 constituent repos). Single-project proof status is
tracked in the owning repos. Requirements: `+PROOF-NEEDS.adoc+`.
____

The machine-readable record of every proof is
`+verification/proofs/LEDGER.json+`. This page is a *summary of that ledger*;
each theorem's presence and documented grade in the summary table are checked
against the ledger, and `+just proof-ledger-check+` fails if they differ.
Nothing here is a percentage, a confidence
estimate, or a count of "done" work: mathesis §6 requires the exact form —
Open / Claimed, the grade, the axiom list, and the commit the receipt was taken
at.

=== The one theorem

[cols="10%,12%,9%,26%,21%,22%",options="header",]
|===
|ID |Kind |Grade |Source leg |Rocq recheck leg |Lean 4 recheck leg
|XP-1 |theory |*2i* |Agda — `+proven+`
(`+EchoTyping.agda+`, `+Verification.agda+`)
|`+proven+` — `+EchoTyping.v+` (`+axioms: []+`) |`+proven+` — `+EchoTyping.lean+` (`+axioms: []+`, no Mathlib)
|===

`+XP-1+` states that the pipeline's information-losing typing operations
(AffineScript affine weakening; refinement erasure into the
typed-wasm/kernel layer) are instances of echo-types' fiber construction.

Four caveats belong to that sentence, and the ledger carries them in
`+caveats+` so the gate can refuse a record that drops one:

. **echo-types' identity claim is open.** What is proven here is a statement
  about *this pipeline's* operations. The claim that `+Echo+` *is* the identity
  of structured loss is echo-types' own, and at the pinned revision it is open
  by design: their README says "Positive identity is open ... Criterion 2 is
  open". Citing XP-1 for that claim would be circular. If echo-types closes
  criterion 2, XP-1 inherits it; until then XP-1 consumes a construction, it does
  not witness the thesis.
. **absolute-zero is a proof-bearing consumer with 61 undischarged axioms** and
  OND-6 open by design. None of its Coq is in this proof's import cone — it
  reaches us only through echo-types' library manifest — but the record must not
  be read as saying that repository is axiom-free.
. **epistemic-types is a sibling, not a dependency.** That repository formalises
  the same object from a different premise: its `EchoBridge.Echo` is a *certified
  residue* (`residue`, `certified : Cert residue y`) over a `Retention` record,
  while the `Echo` used here is echo-types' fibre `Σ A (λ x → f x ≡ y)`. Neither
  imports the other. XP-1 says nothing about epistemic-types' warrant transport
  (`checkerSound`, `proofCannotSupportFalse`, `opaqueNotCertifying`,
  `verifyReceiptIsGap`) or its `ForgedResidue` rejection; the ledger records it
  under `related_developments` with `consumed_by_this_proof: false`, and the gate
  refuses a record that claims otherwise.
. **All three kernels are in the DTT/CIC family.** XP-1 is checked across Agda
  (`+--safe --without-K --cubical-compatible+`), Rocq 8.18 (`+EchoTyping.v+`,
  `+Closed under the global context+`), and core Lean 4.15.0 (`+EchoTyping.lean+`,
  zero axioms, no Mathlib), satisfying `+PROOF-POLICY+` §1–§2 and establishing
  *Grade 2i*. Because Agda, Rocq and Lean all belong to the DTT/CIC family,
  *Grade 2ii* (which requires a second foundational architecture such as
  Isabelle/HOL) and *Grade 1* (verified external checker API receipt) are not
  claimed.

=== What the grade means

mathesis `+estate-mathematical-architecture+` §5 defines the rungs. *Grade 2i*
is established when a theorem's Agda source proof (`+--safe --without-K
--cubical-compatible+`) is independently verified in at least one distinct
DTT system with statement comparison and verbatim kernel assumption receipts.
Here **both** `+PROOF-POLICY+` §2 recheck legs (`+rocq+` and `+lean4+`, zero
Mathlib) are proven with zero axioms.

The rungs above `+2i+` (`+2ii+` = two fundamentally distinct architectures,
`+1+` = machine-checked through a verified checker API) are *not established*:

* `+2i+` is **established** via both `+EchoTyping.v+` (Rocq 8.18) and
  `+EchoTyping.lean+` (core Lean 4.15.0, including Type-valued Martin-Löf
  `+Path+` companions verifying zero reliance on UIP/K).
* `+2ii+` needs a proven leg outside the DTT/CIC family (such as Isabelle/HOL).
  Translating within Agda/Rocq/Lean proves cross-kernel DTT reproducibility,
  not cross-foundational independence.
* `+1+` needs the statement checked through a verified checker API (AXLE for
  Lean, coq-lsp's REST surface for Rocq, isabelle-server). No such surface is
  wired up.

=== Corroboration: the same proof under a second Agda

The receipt is one run of one toolchain. On 2026-11-01 the two modules were also
type-checked in an agent sandbox with a *different* Agda — 2.7.0.1 (PyPI
manylinux wheel, GHC 9.6.5) rather than the pinned 2.8.0 — against agda-stdlib
v2.3 (and again against v2.4), echo-types `+b0982c44+` and absolute-zero
`+f486c29+`, under the same canonical invocation:

[source,bash]
----
agda --safe --without-K --cubical-compatible EchoTyping.agda
agda --safe --without-K --cubical-compatible Verification.agda
----

Both passed, with only the `+-WnoUnsupportedIndexedMatch+` warnings that come
from echo-types' own indexed matches. The environment was shown to reject a
planted `+⊥+` (Agda exit 42) in the same session, so the pass is not vacuous.

This is recorded in the ledger as `+local_evidence+` and it is deliberately
*not* the receipt: §2.1 pins the compiler version with the result, and a run at
a different version cannot carry the grade. It answers a different question —
"is this proof an artifact of one toolchain?" — and the gate refuses a local run
that sets `+corroboration_only+` to anything but true.

=== Moving XP-1 up one rung (to `+2i+`): the runbook

. Pick *one* leg. Two half-done legs are worth less than one done leg: `+2i+`
   needs exactly one.
. Write the witness under `+verification/proofs/coq/+` or
   `+verification/proofs/lean4/+`, with *no Mathlib* and no axiom of convenience.
. Register it in that prover's `+MANIFEST+` as `+gated+`.
. Record the leg in `+LEDGER.json+`: `+status+`, `+route+` (`+transported+` or
   `+re-proved+`), `+artifact+`, `+axioms+` from `+Print Assumptions+` (Rocq) or
   `+#print axioms+` (Lean), `+statement_compared+` against the declarations in
   `+statement_formal+`, and the receipt commit.
. Record the promotion in `+history+`: `+{target: "grade", from: "3", to: "2i",
   evidence: <run>, ...}+`. The gate requires the last history entry for a target
   to equal that target's current value, so history cannot be rewritten.
. Register the file in both registries (`+[verification].allowed-proofs+` and
   `+ALLOWED_PROOFS+`) and run `+just proof-witness-check+`.

What will *not* be accepted as reaching `+2i+`: a compiler exit code with no
axiom query (Rocq accepts `+Admitted+`, Lean accepts `+sorry+`); a leg marked
`+proven+` with no artifact, no MANIFEST entry, no route, no statement
comparison or no axiom list; a Mathlib import; or a grade raised while the legs
stay `+open+`. Each of those is a control in `+tests/proofs/+`.

=== Regressions and supersession

mathesis §6 requires a proof's history to say when it is superseded or
regresses. The ledger's `+history+` array is checked rather than decorative:

* every entry needs a date, a target (`+grade+` or a prover), a `+from+`, a
  `+to+` and the evidence that showed the transition;
* the *last* entry for a target must equal that target's current value — a
  downgrade cannot be quietly applied, and a record cannot be quietly dropped;
* a transition to a weaker state (a grade moving down the ladder, or a leg
  returning to `+open+`) must carry a `+reason+`.

An honest, evidenced regression passes: the rule cuts both ways, and
`+tests/proofs/run-ledger-controls.sh+` control 7 pins that.

=== Waivers

**No waiver is claimed for XP-1, and none is recorded in the ledger.** §4 of
`+3-practice/PROOF-POLICY.adoc+` waives a leg when the mapped prover cannot
*express* the statement — not when the estate has simply not built the witness
yet. The Rocq and Lean legs are absent for the second reason, so they are open
obligations, not waivers, and a waiver would be the wrong instrument even if it
were convenient.

The ledger half of the rule is mechanical, and it is deliberately hard to
satisfy with a citation that does not exist. A waiver must carry an
`+obligation+`, a `+tool+`, a `+reason+` and a `+record+`; the `+record+` must be
`+<path>[#<anchor>]+` inside this repository, the path must exist, the anchor
must occur in the file, and the file must name the `+tool+`. Anything else — a
bare string, a plausible-looking filename, an anchor this page does not have —
is refused, because a waiver whose record cannot be read is an obligation
removed without a reason. Controls 26, 37 and 38 in
`+tests/proofs/run-ledger-controls.sh+` pin the three cases: a conforming
waiver is accepted and printed in the verdict, an unresolvable record is
refused, and an anchor that does not occur in the cited file is refused.

This section is itself the anchor that a waiver record would cite
(`+PROOF-STATUS.adoc#waivers+`), which is why it exists before the first waiver
does.

=== Assumptions and axiom tracking

mathesis §6 requires the axioms behind a proof to be tracked, not assumed away:
`+#print axioms+` in Lean, `+Print Assumptions+` in Rocq. Those are kernel
queries, and they are the only accepted evidence that a compiled witness is
closed — compiling is not: Rocq accepts `+Admitted+` and Lean accepts `+sorry+`
with exit status 0. Since no witness exists on either leg there is no axiom
list for them yet; recording one would be fabrication.

**Enforced per compiled witness (issue #131 closed):** `+scripts/check-proofs.sh+`
does not stop at compiler exit status 0. Every `+gated+` Rocq or Lean module in
`+MANIFEST+` is compiled in an isolated scratch workspace and immediately
subjected to kernel-level assumption queries compared against the claiming leg's
`+axioms+` array in `+verification/proofs/LEDGER.json+`:

* **Rocq (`+coq+`):** runs `+coqchk -Q . "" -silent -o <module>+` on the
  compiled `+.vo+` (requiring predicative `+Set+`, `+<none>+` for
  `+type-in-type+`, unsafe `+(co)fixpoints+`, and assumed positivity, and
  matching `+* Axioms:+` against `+legs[].axioms+`) *and* compiles a generated
  probe that `+Require+`s `+<module>+` and executes `+Print Assumptions
  <module>.<decl>.+` for every mapped declaration, failing on any undeclared or
  stale axiom — including standard-library or cross-module imported axioms that
  carry zero banned lexical tokens.
* **Lean 4 (`+lean4+`):** compiles `+<module>.olean+` and runs a generated probe
  that `+import+`s `+<module>+` and executes `+#print axioms <decl>+` for every
  mapped declaration, unconditionally rejecting `+sorryAx+` (even across module
  imports or if listed in `+axioms+`) and failing on any undeclared or stale
  ledger axiom.
* **Verbatim receipts:** `+just proof-check-coq+` and `+just proof-check-lean4+`
  write the verbatim `+coqchk+`, `+Print Assumptions+`, and `+#print axioms+`
  outputs into `+target/proof-receipts/witness-coq.txt+` and
  `+target/proof-receipts/witness-lean4.txt+` (uploaded by `+proof-ci.yml+`).

These are not the same kind of entry, and the ledger says so: a *dependency-pin*
is a version, an *external development* is another repository whose own
obligations have to be recorded alongside it, and a *proof-bearing consumer*
consumes the estate's proof infrastructure rather than providing ground truth for
it. Three things follow, all enforced by the ledger gate:

. A dependency that carries open obligations must be named in the theorem's
  `caveats`. A theorem that lists 61 undischarged axioms against its own name
  without saying so in prose would be read as axiom-free.
. The **import cone** is recorded, module by module, with its `{-# OPTIONS #-}`
  flags and postulate counts (`+source.import_cone+`). `+--safe+` refuses an
  import that is not itself safe, so that cone *is* the trust base for the "no
  escape hatches" claim. At the pin it is `Echo`, `EchoCharacteristic` and
  `EchoLinear`, all `+--safe --without-K --cubical-compatible+`, all with zero
  postulates. The postulated modules in echo-types (`+EchoImageFactorizationPropPostulated+`,
  `+Ordinal/Buchholz/Fidelity+`) are outside `+All.agda+` and outside this cone;
  they are recorded as outside, not omitted.
. A claim about another repository needs evidence — the file and the date it was
  read — and the gate refuses one that has none.

The current caveats are listed under "The Grade 3 claim rests on". They are part
of the record, not decoration: the gate fails if an open obligation loses its
caveat.

receipts; witnesses importing `+Coq.Logic.Classical_Prop.classic+` or
`+Classical.em+` (clean to lexical scan) must fail under `+axioms: []+` and pass
under `+proven-with-postulates+` naming the exact axioms; cross-module helper
axioms, cross-module `+sorryAx+`, `+Admitted+` theorems listed in `+.axioms+`,
out-of-order multi-file `+MANIFEST+` entries, missing declarations, and stale
`+axioms+` entries must all be refused. Where `+coqc+`, `+coqchk+`, `+coqtop+`, or `+lean+`
is absent the kernel half reports **BLOCKED**, never a pass.

|`+just proof-grade+`
|Prints, per theorem, what the recorded grade rests on and the exact delta to
the next rung, computed from the ledger — so "how far off `+2i+` are we?" is
answered by the record rather than by prose.

|`+just proof-enrolment+`
(`+scripts/check-proof-enrolment.sh+`)
|Parses `+[verification].allowed-proofs+` (placement.a2ml) and
`+ALLOWED_PROOFS+` (`+scripts/check-coordination-boundary.sh+`) and requires
them to agree. Fails when a proof source is unlisted, when a registered entry
has no file behind it, when zero proofs are enrolled, or when an `.agda` proof
loses `+--safe+`/`+--without-K+`.

|`+just proof-check-agda+` (and the Idris2/Lean4/Coq equivalents)
|Exits 1 (`+FAIL (missing-toolchain)+`) when proofs for that prover are enrolled
but the prover is absent, and exits 1 on any typecheck or kernel-assumption failure.

|`+just proof-scan-dangerous+`
(`+scripts/proof-scan-dangerous.sh+`)
|Comment-aware keyword scan for `+believe_me+`, `+assert_total+`,
`+postulate+`, `+sorry+`, `+Admitted+`, `+unsafeCoerce+`, `+Obj.magic+`. Fails
on any hit outside a comment, and fails when it scans zero files.

|`.github/workflows/proof-ci.yml`
|Hosted lane running `+just proof-witness-check+` (plus the Agda source leg and
the both-ways controls) on pushes/PRs that touch `+verification/proofs/**+`,
`+tests/proofs/**+`, the two registries, the gate scripts, `+Justfile+` or the
workflow itself. Pins Agda 2.8.0 (official binary, sha256-verified),
agda-stdlib v2.4 (tag + commit asserted), `+just+` 1.59.0, Rocq, Lean 4, and
echo-types / absolute-zero by commit. No `+continue-on-error+`, no
skip-on-missing-tool.
